SUBJECT: Hooks + opening lines for AFTER THE BEEP funeral cold-open (ranked)

Per lane ask: 5 first-3-seconds hook variants + 3 opening-line options. First frame must read muted.

=== RANKED FIRST-3-SECONDS HOOK VARIANTS ===
(All start on the muted visual of the phone lighting up with Daniel’s photo as caller ID amid the funeral reception. Voiceover hits immediately after the visual lock.)

1. TOP: Extreme close-up of Mara’s hand holding a black phone. Screen flares on. Caller ID: Daniel’s smiling face. Timestamp overlay: THREE DAYS AFTER. Cut to her face going still as the room keeps moving behind her.
   Why: Instant “dead man calling” read. No text needed.

2. Wide of the reception (black clothes, lilies, quiet crowd). Phone in her pocket lights the fabric from inside. She pulls it out — Daniel’s photo fills the screen. Everyone else still talking.
   Why: Contrast of ordinary grief vs. the impossible call.

3. Over-the-shoulder: Mara staring at the closed casket. Phone in her other hand vibrates and lights. Caller photo is Daniel. She doesn’t look at it for a beat — then does.
   Why: Delayed recognition sells the shock.

4. Phone already in hand, screen off. It powers on by itself. Daniel’s face. She almost drops it. Background blur of a relative reaching for her shoulder.
   Why: The phone acts on its own — uncanny.

5. Split: left side casket flowers, right side phone screen with Daniel’s photo sliding in as the call arrives. Her thumb hovers, then freezes.
   Why: Clean visual juxtaposition for muted viewing.

=== RANKED OPENING-LINE OPTIONS ===
(For the first voicemail that plays over the phone visual. Keep under 8 words for the hook window.)

1. TOP: “Tomorrow, 8:04. Don’t take the elevator.”
   Why: Specific, actionable, immediately wrong for a dead man.

2. “Don’t go home tonight. It’s already there.”
   Why: Personal dread, no time to process.

3. “You buried the wrong one.”
   Why: Blunt identity crack. Highest risk/reward.

All hooks assume the first frame is the phone lighting up with his face — readable with sound off. Ready for your script draft.
