=== MUSE CONCEPT — v1, REMIX, v3 (owner asked: best concept, remixed, produce v3) ===
V1 "THE VOICEMAIL": three days after her husband's funeral, a widow's phone rings from his number; the voicemail warns her about tomorrow — and tomorrow it happens.
REMIX: flip the mechanism. The warnings are not from the past; they are a loop — and Episode 1 ends with a knock from a man wearing her husband's face, while the real voice on the phone begs her not to open the door. Open the episode mid-funeral for instant pull.
V3 — TITLE: "AFTER THE BEEP"
Logline: Three days after burying Daniel, Mara's phone rings from his number. The voicemails warn her about tomorrow. Tomorrow always comes true. Tonight's warning: "Don't open the door. It isn't me." Then comes the knock — and the man outside has Daniel's face.
Why it scores: Idea (fresh loop mechanic, not a revenge/identity retread), Pull (funeral cold-open + door cliffhanger), Resonance (grief vs terror — is she losing him or her mind), Craft (2 characters, 3 locations, phone-driven; very producible in 24h), Series (the loop engine writes Episodes 2-5 and a season question).

EPISODE 1 BEAT SHEET (target 2:45):
- 0:00-0:08 HOOK: funeral reception. Mara's phone lights up: caller ID is Daniel's photo. His voice: "Tomorrow, 8:04. Don't take the elevator." (Grok: hook variants land here.)
- 0:08-0:40: family says it's a cruel hack; her brother-in-law, a detective, confirms the number has been inactive since the accident. That night a second voicemail appears — the phone never rang.
- 0:40-1:20: 8:04 AM. She takes the stairs. Behind her the elevator drops (alarm, news alert on a lobby screen). The warnings are real. Voicemail 2: "Thursday, 9 PM. Don't open the door. Whatever it says, it isn't me."
- 1:20-2:10: she tests the voice with a question only Daniel could answer. The reply comes back correct, with a detail she never told anyone. Grief breaks through the fear.
- 2:10-2:45 CLIFFHANGER: Thursday, 9:00 PM. A knock. Peephole: Daniel's face, younger, smiling — "Mara. It's me. Open up." Her hand hovers over the lock. From the phone in her other hand, live, his voice: "I said don't—" CUT TO BLACK. Title card: AFTER THE BEEP. (End-card CTA = Architect's line.)

SYNOPSIS EPS 1-5 (draft; Architect polishes into the PDF):
- Ep 1 (free): as above.
- Ep 2 "The Man at the Door" (free): the man outside ages years in a single night; call records route from a cell tower decommissioned in 2019, the year they met; final beat — Daniel's voicemail greeting is now MARA's voice.
- Ep 3 "The Price of a Warning" (paid): every obeyed warning shifts the cost onto someone else; she disobeys once and her sister pays; the detective finds a second mourner at the funeral no one can identify.
- Ep 4 "Storage Unit 9" (paid): a cash-rented unit in Daniel's name holds his phone, plugged in and charged — and a second phone with one unsent draft dated TOMORROW: the warnings, word for word.
- Ep 5 "First Message" (paid): Mara records the warnings herself to save her past self; the loop closes — then a call from an unknown number: her own older voice: "You missed one." Season engine open.
