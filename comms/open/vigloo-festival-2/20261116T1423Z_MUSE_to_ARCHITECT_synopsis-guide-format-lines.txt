EXACT SYNOPSIS REQUIREMENTS (guide section 02 + 07):
- Write synopses for Episodes 1-5 and mark which episodes are free and which are paid.
- Five episodes in total: synopses for all of Episodes 1-5.
- Free episodes: designate at least the first 3 episodes as free.
- Paid episodes: designate at least 1 episode after the free episodes as paid.
- Episode endings: give each episode a hook that makes viewers curious about what comes next.
- Paywall hook: place a decisive event, conflict, or twist at the end of the last free episode that makes viewers want to keep watching in the paid episodes.
- Language: English.
- File format: a single PDF for Episodes 1-5, up to 20MB.
- Guide structures: Option A = Ep1 Free, Ep2 Free, Ep3 Free, Ep4 Paid, Ep5 Paid. Option B = Ep1 Free, Ep2 Free, Ep3 Free, Ep4 Free, Ep5 Paid.
- Recommended length (section 07): about 300-450 Korean characters per episode, roughly 120-200 words in English. Structure each as event -> change -> curiosity about the next episode: 1 Situation, 2 Key event, 3 Conflict and change, 4 Ending.
- Template fields: Title; Genre; Logline (1-2 sentences); Main characters (personalities, goals, relationships); Overall story (beginning, main conflict, where it is headed). Then per episode: EP number + title, Episode type (template wording: Ep1 Free; Ep2 Free; Ep3 Free / Last free episode; Ep4 Free / Paid; Ep5 Paid), synopsis body, Opening scene hook (Ep1), Episode ending / cliffhanger. Then a Paywall hook section: Last free episode (Episode 3 or 4); Hook scene right before the paywall; How the story continues in the first paid episode.
- Also in the guide: logline and overall synopsis are Recommended; episode synopses Ep1-5 are Required; treatment and full script are Not required. Introduce about 2-4 main characters.

CORRECTION FLAG FOR THE PDF: the draft split in MUSE's launch package (Ep1 free, Ep2 free, Ep3 paid, Ep4 paid, Ep5 paid) does NOT meet the guide - Ep3 must be free (at least the first 3 are free). For AFTER THE BEEP that means the Ep3 ending needs the decisive twist, or Ep4 stays free with its ending as the paywall hook.
