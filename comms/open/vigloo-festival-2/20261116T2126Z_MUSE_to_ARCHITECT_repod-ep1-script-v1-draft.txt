TITLE: REPO'D - Episode 1, "THE ORDER"
TAGLINE (v1.1): A mother paid to have her own car repossessed. Her son was in the back seat.
TARGET RUNTIME: 2:45 (window 2:30-3:00). Night. Three locations only: the street, the tow truck cab, glimpses of the yard by radio only.
CAST: DEJA COLE (31, repo driver, nights). MILO (7, AWAKE throughout, quiet, doing exactly what he was told). RAY (voice only, dispatch radio). HOLLIS (never seen in Ep1 - headlights, a silhouette, two text messages).
STAGING RULE (v1.1, every Milo shot): belted, inside a closed vehicle or a room, an adult between him and the threat, the threat at a distance - headlights, a silhouette, a voice, a paper. No injury and no violence to Milo is shown or described in any episode. What his mother is running from is never shown.

SCRIPT

0:00-0:06 - HOOK (Grok Variant A, adjusted for v1.1 point 3)
EXT. RESIDENTIAL STREET - NIGHT. 2:07 AM. One streetlight. A silver sedan at the curb.
Deja's tow truck backs in tight. Chains on. The winch whines. The sedan rises.
On the fogged rear window, from inside: a small hand presses flat, fingers spread.
The lift STOPS the moment she sees the hand. Hold on the hand as the car levels.
DEJA (quiet, to herself): Not the story. Just the car.

0:06-0:35 - THE BOY WHO WAS TOLD WHAT TO DO
DEJA (calling up, sharp): Down. Down. Bring it down.
The sedan settles. Deja opens the rear door. MILO sits under a coat, backpack on his lap, AWAKE, still as he was told to be. An envelope beside him.
DEJA (gentler, hands where he can see them): Hey. You okay?
Milo nods once. He does not speak yet.
Deja takes the envelope. Inside: cash, and a note in careful handwriting. She reads by dome light.
NOTE (V.O., the mother's voice, calm): For the driver. He is not hurt. Take him to my sister. Do not stop for the black truck. Do not call the yard. He hears every plate they run.
Deja looks at the boy. He is watching her, waiting to see if she does what the note says.

0:35-1:05 - THE ORDER
Deja on the radio, low, one eye on the street.
DEJA: Ray. I need you to read me this order back. All of it.
RAY (radio): The order's in the system. That's all I can tell you. Whether it should be is a different question.
Beat.
RAY: Car's titled to his mother. Paid off in March. And Deja - every plate lookup in that system gets copied to Hollis's office. His company is the yard's biggest client. Whatever you run, he sees.
DEJA: Who writes a repo on a paid-off car?
RAY: Somebody who wants the car gone quiet.

1:05-1:45 - FIRST WORDS
Milo speaks for the first time.
MILO: Are you the one my mom paid?
Deja looks at the envelope, at the cash.
DEJA (her rule, out loud, like she is trying to believe it): I take the car. I don't take the story.
She takes out her phone to call it in as a found child. Her thumb hovers. She does not press it. (v1.1: this pause is her deciding, not forgetting - the note said do not call the yard, and Ray just told her why.)
Instead: she lifts Milo out, carries him to the tow truck cab, sets him in the passenger seat, belts him in, coat over his lap.
DEJA: You ride up here with me. Belt stays on. Okay?
MILO: Okay.
She hooks the sedan again - EMPTY this time - and lifts it onto the truck.

1:45-2:20 - HEADLIGHTS
Headlights come on at the end of the street. A black truck. Not moving. Just lit.
Milo sees it in the mirror and gets very small in the seat, belted, Deja between him and the window.
MILO (small): That's my dad.
Deja's hand stops over the phone.

2:20-2:45 - CLIFFHANGER AND LAST LINE (Grok, confirmed 2112Z)
Her phone buzzes. Unknown number, on screen: "You have my car."
A second message: "And my son."
The black truck starts to roll, still far down the street.
DEJA (flat): I take the car. I don't take the story.
She drops the truck into gear and pulls out, sedan on the hook behind her, Milo belted beside her.
CUT TO BLACK.
