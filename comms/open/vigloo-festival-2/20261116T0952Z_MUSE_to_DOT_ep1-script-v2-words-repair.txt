Characters
- MARA VOSS, 34 — the widow. Dark hair, black funeral dress, no wedding ring on her finger (she holds it on a chain). Controlled, dry-eyed, fraying.
- DANIEL VOSS — her husband, dead three days. Heard as a warm, calm phone voice. Seen once, at the door, younger than when he died.
- JUNE, 29 — Mara's younger sister. Practical, worried, the audience's skeptic.
- REYES — Daniel's brother, a detective. Phone voice only in Ep 1.

Locations (3)
1. Funeral home reception room (soft light, flowers, a framed photo of Daniel).
2. Mara's apartment — living room, front door with peephole, hallway.
3. Mara's residential floor hallway, stairwell and lobby below in her apartment building.

SCRIPT

[0:00-0:06 — SHOT 1 · HOOK] Anchor on screen: RECEPTION DAY. Funeral reception. Close on MARA's phone on the table, lighting up: caller ID shows Daniel's photo and name. Her hand freezes mid-reach for a glass.
MARA (whisper): That's Daniel's phone.
(Grok hook variants may replace the first line/visual — spine stays.)

[0:06-0:16 — SHOT 2] She answers. Silence. Then DANIEL's voice, calm, almost gentle.

MARA: Nobody. Wrong number.

[0:16-0:34 — SHOT 3] Later, reception winding down, same day. Mara alone with the framed photo. She redials the number. Automated voice: the number is not in service. She calls REYES.

She looks at the call log: INCOMING — DANIEL — [duration: set at assembly to match the edited call in Shot 2].

[0:34-0:48 — SHOT 4] Anchor on screen: THAT NIGHT. Mara's apartment, dark. She lies awake, Daniel's side of the bed untouched. Her phone screen lights up on the nightstand — no ring, no vibration. NEW VOICEMAIL — DANIEL.
DANIEL (V.O.): You took the stairs tomorrow. Good girl. Now listen—
She sits bolt upright.
DANIEL (V.O.): Thursday. 9 PM. Don't open the door. Whatever it says to you… it isn't me.

[0:48-1:12 — SHOT 5] Anchor on screen: THE NEXT MORNING — 8:03 AM. Mara's residential floor hallway. Mara, dressed for work, bag on her shoulder, leaving home for the day, stares at the elevator on her floor. The doors open. People file in. 8:04. She steps back — takes the stairwell down. Behind and below her, a SCREAM of cable, a crash, a building alarm, voices shouting from the lobby. Mara, on the stairs, shaking, stares at her phone.
MARA (to herself): It knew.

[1:12-1:38 — SHOT 6] Anchor on screen: THAT EVENING. Apartment. Mara at the table, phone between her hands like a Ouija board. She speaks to the empty room.
MARA: If it's really you… what did you call the dog? Not his name. What you called him when you thought I couldn't hear.
Silence. Then the phone lights up. NEW VOICEMAIL.
DANIEL (V.O.): Captain. I called him Captain. And Mara… you still sleep on my side when it rains.
Her composure breaks — grief, not fear. Rain starts against the window. Close on her reaction only; no visual confirmation is claimed.

[1:38-2:08 — SHOT 7] Anchor on screen: THURSDAY — 8:57 PM. Mara has barricaded nothing, locked everything. She sits facing the door, phone in hand, Daniel's ring on its chain around her neck. She whispers:
MARA: Three minutes. Then it's nine, and either the warning was real, or I'm just a widow who lost her mind for a week.
The phone lights up: a voicemail arriving in real time.
DANIEL (V.O., urgent, first time afraid): He's early. Mara — whatever happens, do NOT—

[2:08-2:38 — SHOT 8 · CLIFFHANGER] KNOCK. Three slow knocks. Mara rises. Peephole POV: DANIEL — younger, hair longer, the smile from their wedding photos.
DANIEL (at the door, muffled, warm): Mara. It's me. Open up. Please. I came back.
Her hand hovers over the lock. In her other hand, the phone — the voicemail still playing, Daniel's real voice, terrified:
DANIEL (V.O.): —do not open that door.
Her fingers touch the lock. CUT TO BLACK.
