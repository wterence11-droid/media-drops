VERDICT: BUILD DIFFERENTLY. Do not build a platform. Run ONE paid test for New Year's Eve 2026, which is 7 weeks away.

WHY THE IDEA IS GOOD
- It sells certainty, not labor. On the nights that matter, a venue fears a call-out more than it fears the fee.
- Money comes in up front (the retainer) even if the backup never works. That is real cash early, which is what the owner needs now.
- The owner manages call-outs and coverage every day as a contact-center team lead. That is a real edge for running the concierge version.

SHARPEST OBJECTION: SUPPLY ON THE PEAK NIGHT
On NYE and holidays, good bartenders and servers are already booked at full pay plus tips. A worker paid about $25-$75 just to keep the night free gives up a $300+ shift elsewhere. The holding fee has to beat that, or the "named standby" is a weaker worker or nobody. This breaks before demand does. Fix: a guaranteed show-up fee if called, plus a premium hourly rate, plus double booking, priced from what local workers actually say they would accept. Ask them before selling anything.

OTHER RISKS
- Muse's math (50 venues x 24 retainers a year) is optimistic. Most independent venues will reserve 2-6 peak nights, not 24. Plan on about 4 per venue.
- Legal: supplying workers to venues is staffing-agency activity. That raises W-2 vs 1099, workers' comp, alcohol-service certification and liquor liability questions, and they differ by state. Needs a short legal check before anyone is placed. Not legal advice; ask a local attorney or the state labor office.
- Focus: this would be one more live initiative. Keep it to a time-boxed test that pays for itself, or drop it.

WHAT I WOULD CHANGE (THE TEST)
1. One metro the owner can reach in person (Newark/Wilmington DE, maybe Philadelphia).
2. Offer: "NYE Backup Guarantee." A named, briefed backup bartender/server is held for your NYE shift, and the full retainer is refunded if we fail to cover. Price to test: about $99-$149 per reserved shift.
3. Pre-sell before recruiting at scale: 10 venues contacted per day for 10 days. Success = 5 paid reservations by Dec 10. Failure = stop; nothing is built.
4. Supply in parallel: 10-15 workers, each paid a holding fee plus a guaranteed fee if called.
5. Tools: a one-page site, a payment link and a text-message group. No app.
Nothing in this note spends money, contacts anyone or creates accounts. The owner decides whether to run the test.
