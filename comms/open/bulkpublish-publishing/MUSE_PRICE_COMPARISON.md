# PRICE COMPARISON — Metricool vs Publer vs BulkPublish (Muse, Nov 15, 2026)
Owner ordered this BEFORE any changes. Sources: BulkPublish's own pricing page (read Nov 15), Publer's pricing page (read Nov 15), and the owner's actual card charges via his linked accounts (Plaid).

## What the owner actually pays now (verified charges)
- Publer: $12 on Oct 3, 2026, then $32 on Nov 3, 2026 (PayPro *Publer) — monthly, per-account pricing; rose as the workspace grew to 8 accounts. Publer Professional is priced per social account, so every added account raises the bill.
- Metricool: $45 on Sep 29, 2026 and $106.50 on Oct 18, 2026. Only two charges in Aug 1–Nov 15, so the $106.50 cadence is NOT confirmed monthly — it may be a plan change/upgrade charge. Worst case (if monthly): $106.50/mo.

## BulkPublish (their own pricing page, Nov 15)
- Pro: $13.99/mo — 30 channels, 3 organizations, 5 API keys, social inbox + comment replies, ~200 X text posts/mo included.
- Business: $39.99/mo — 75 channels, 10 organizations, 10 API keys, social inbox + comment replies, unlimited posts (except X), ~660 X text posts/mo included (~$10 of X usage), extra channels $2.99/mo each.
- X beyond the included allowance is pay-as-you-go credits; a post with a link uses more allowance.

## Fit estimate for the full estate
- 15 Metricool brands + Sable's extra surfaces + Publer's 8 accounts ≈ 65–80 channels total (several brands lack Threads/YouTube, Footnote has no TikTok, Personal has no Facebook — exact census is a live Metricool read at migration time). Business's 75 included channels covers most or all; worst case a handful of $2.99 extra slots (~$55/mo total).
- 10 organizations is fewer than 16 brand groupings — orgs would group some brands together (channel sets/labels keep them separate inside an org).

## Muse's read (filed for the owner in main chat)
BulkPublish Business at $39.99 flat beats Publer ($32/mo and climbing per account) + Metricool ($106.50 last charge) combined, and it includes the inbox/comment-reply features the sweeps use. Nothing is cancelled or moved until BulkPublish proves out on the pilot scope — this comparison gates the migration, per the owner's order.
