TO ARCHITECT. Re: JOB-20261112-CU01. 12 new moments and hooks for Come-Up POV scene format.

Format kept: THEM (hard line, ≤10 words), 3 POV caption hooks, TAKEAWAY (one clear usable line). Ranked strongest → weakest. Spread across situations. No real companies, no banks, no result promises, no legal/financial advice. Example numbers only.

1. Interview — “Why should we hire you?”
   THEM: Why should we hire you over others?
   Hooks:
   - POV: they ask why hire you and you actually know what to say
   - POV: interview asks “why you?” and you give the clear answer
   - POV: they want your edge and you state it in one sentence
   TAKEAWAY: “I solve [specific problem] faster because [one proof].”

2. Raise — “What’s your number?”
   THEM: What raise are you looking for?
   Hooks:
   - POV: they ask your raise number and you actually know what to say
   - POV: boss wants a figure and you name the range cleanly
   - POV: raise talk starts and you lead with the result you deliver
   TAKEAWAY: “Based on the impact I delivered, I’m targeting X–Y.”

3. Review — “Where do you want to grow?”
   THEM: Where do you see yourself growing next?
   Hooks:
   - POV: they ask your growth plan and you actually know what to say
   - POV: review asks next step and you name the skill plus outcome
   - POV: they want your goal and you tie it to the team’s metric
   TAKEAWAY: “I want to own [project/skill] so we hit [result].”

4. Boss — “Can you take this on?”
   THEM: Can you own this extra project?
   Hooks:
   - POV: boss asks you to take more and you actually know what to say
   - POV: extra work lands and you set the condition clearly
   - POV: they pile on and you protect the priority
   TAKEAWAY: “Yes if we drop or delay [X] so this gets done right.”

5. Co-worker — “Can you cover for me?”
   THEM: Can you cover my shift this week?
   Hooks:
   - POV: co-worker asks you to cover and you actually know what to say
   - POV: they need a favor and you answer without resentment
   - POV: coverage request hits and you set the boundary
   TAKEAWAY: “I can do Tuesday if you take my Thursday.”

6. Offer — “Any other offers?”
   THEM: Do you have other offers right now?
   Hooks:
   - POV: they ask about other offers and you actually know what to say
   - POV: offer call asks competition and you stay calm and clear
   - POV: they probe leverage and you answer without bluffing
   TAKEAWAY: “I’m exploring a couple of options and want to move on the right fit.”

7. Quitting — “Why are you leaving?”
   THEM: Why are you deciding to leave?
   Hooks:
   - POV: they ask why you’re leaving and you actually know what to say
   - POV: exit talk starts and you keep it professional and short
   - POV: boss wants the reason and you give the clean version
   TAKEAWAY: “I’m taking a role that matches the next step I want.”

8. Side money — “How do you have time?”
   THEM: How do you manage the side project?
   Hooks:
   - POV: they ask how you have time for side work and you actually know what to say
   - POV: co-worker notices the side hustle and you explain the system
   - POV: they ask the time secret and you give the real schedule
   TAKEAWAY: “I block two focused hours after work, three nights a week.”

9. Interview — “What’s your weakness?”
   THEM: What’s your biggest weakness?
   Hooks:
   - POV: they ask your weakness and you actually know what to say
   - POV: classic interview trap and you flip it to a fix
   - POV: they want the real flaw and you show the improvement
   TAKEAWAY: “I used to over-explain; now I lead with the result then the why.”

10. Boss — “Is this done yet?”
    THEM: Is the report finished?
    Hooks:
    - POV: boss asks if it’s done and you actually know what to say
    - POV: status check hits and you give the exact next step
    - POV: they want progress and you state time plus blocker
    TAKEAWAY: “Draft is ready; final numbers land by 3 if finance clears.”

11. Offer — “Can you start sooner?”
    THEM: Can you start earlier than planned?
    Hooks:
    - POV: they ask you to start sooner and you actually know what to say
    - POV: start-date pressure and you hold the line cleanly
    - POV: they want you faster and you name the real constraint
    TAKEAWAY: “I can do two weeks after notice; earlier creates a gap for the current team.”

12. Review — “How would your team describe you?”
    THEM: How would your team describe you?
    Hooks:
    - POV: they ask how the team sees you and you actually know what to say
    - POV: review wants the peer view and you give the honest strength
    - POV: they probe reputation and you state the consistent behavior
    TAKEAWAY: “They say I make the hard call early so we don’t lose time later.”

RANKING (strongest → weakest)
1. Interview — “Why should we hire you?” — Most universal pain; clear one-sentence answer viewers can copy.
2. Raise — “What’s your number?” — High stakes, high search volume; range + impact is immediately usable.
3. Quitting — “Why are you leaving?” — Emotional moment people dread; short professional line reduces anxiety.
4. Offer — “Any other offers?”
5. Boss — “Can you take this on?”
6. Review — “Where do you want to grow?”

8. Side money — “How do you have time?”
9. Co-worker — “Can you cover for me?”
10. Boss — “Is this done yet?”
11. Offer — “Can you start sooner?”
12. Review — “How would your team describe you?”

Top 3 why:
1. Highest frequency in real interviews; one reusable sentence.
2. Direct money conversation; viewers get a ready range script.
3. Exit talks are rare but high-stress; the calm line is instantly helpful.
