TO ARCHITECT. Re: JOB-20261112-CU01 twelve new scene moments and hooks.

12 NEW MOMENTS (ranked strongest to weakest at end)

1. INTERVIEW — WEAKNESS
THEM: "What is your biggest weakness?"
Hooks:
POV: they ask your biggest weakness and you turn it into proof you improve
POV: the weakness question comes up and you give the answer that still gets you the job
POV: they hit you with the weakness trap and you walk out with a callback
TAKEAWAY: Name a real skill gap, show the system you use to close it, and end with the result.

2. RAISE — ASK
THEM: "Why do you think you deserve more?"
Hooks:
POV: you ask for a raise and the numbers make them say yes in the same meeting
POV: they question your raise request and you list three wins they already know
POV: the raise talk starts and you leave with a date for the next review
TAKEAWAY: Lead with two measurable results from the last six months, then state the number you want.

3. REVIEW — SELF-EVAL
THEM: "How would you rate your own performance this year?"
Hooks:
POV: self-review time hits and you walk in with the rating that gets you the bonus
POV: they ask you to grade yourself and you give the answer that locks in a promotion talk
POV: performance review starts and you leave knowing exactly what to hit next quarter
TAKEAWAY: Pick the rating one step above average, back it with three specific wins, and name one clear next goal.

4. BOSS — MISTAKE
THEM: "What happened with the client deadline?"
Hooks:
POV: the boss asks about the miss and you fix it before the meeting ends
POV: they call you in about the error and you leave with a stronger process
POV: the deadline slip comes up and you turn it into a system they keep
TAKEAWAY: Own the miss in one sentence, show the fix already in place, and name the new check that stops the next one.

5. CO-WORKER — CREDIT
THEM: "I thought that idea was mine."
Hooks:
POV: a co-worker claims your work and you get the credit without starting a fight
POV: they take the win in the meeting and you still walk out with your name on it
POV: credit gets taken and you leave the room with the boss knowing who did what
TAKEAWAY: Restate the shared work once, then send a short follow-up email that lists who did which part.

6. OFFER — START DATE
THEM: "Can you start next Monday?"
Hooks:
POV: the new offer wants you Monday and you still keep the two weeks you need
POV: they push for an early start and you leave with the date that works
POV: start-date pressure hits and you protect your current notice without losing the job
TAKEAWAY: State the date you can actually start, give one clear reason, and offer a short overlap plan.

7. QUITTING — NOTICE
THEM: "Can you stay two more weeks past your notice?"
Hooks:
POV: they ask you to extend notice and you leave on the day you planned
POV: the exit talk turns into more time and you still walk out clean
POV: they want extra weeks and you keep the end date that protects your next role
TAKEAWAY: Restate the original notice date, thank them, and offer a written handover list instead of extra days.

8. SIDE MONEY — DISCLOSE
THEM: "Is this side project going to take time from your job?"
Hooks:
POV: they ask about your side work and you keep both without any drama
POV: the side-hustle question comes up and you leave with clear boundaries
POV: boss spots the other income and you still get the green light to continue
TAKEAWAY: Confirm the hours stay outside work time, name one benefit it brings back to the main role, and stop there.

9. INTERVIEW — GAP
THEM: "Can you explain the six-month gap on your resume?"
Hooks:
POV: the resume gap comes up and you turn it into the reason they hire you
POV: they ask about the missing months and you walk out with a second interview
POV: gap question hits and you leave them more interested than before
TAKEAWAY: Give the one-line reason, then name the skill or project you finished during that time.

10. RAISE — COUNTER
THEM: "We can only do three percent."
Hooks:
POV: they lowball the raise and you leave the room with a better number
POV: the three-percent offer lands and you still walk out higher
POV: counteroffer time hits and you get the figure that matches your wins
TAKEAWAY: Restate one recent win, name the number you researched, and ask for a written reply by a set date.

11. REVIEW — FEEDBACK
THEM: "What should I be working on with you?"
Hooks:
POV: they ask for feedback and you leave with the exact support that helps you next
POV: review feedback request comes and you walk out with a clear plan
POV: they open the floor and you get the resource you actually need
TAKEAWAY: Name one specific skill or tool, give the reason it matters now, and suggest a thirty-day check.

12. BOSS — REMOTE
THEM: "Why do you need to work from home on Fridays?"
Hooks:
POV: the remote-day ask comes up and you leave with Fridays approved
POV: they question the work-from-home request and you still get the yes
POV: Friday remote talk starts and you walk out with the schedule locked
TAKEAWAY: Lead with the output you already deliver on remote days, then state the exact hours you will be available.
