   "Could you aim for the earlier bus for the next two weeks?" -> action=false, specific=false, commitment=false
   "So: earlier bus for the next two weeks, and we check in next Friday." -> action=false (specific and commitment are true)
   Cause: the action pattern only knows "going forward / let's agree / next step / from Monday..." and so on, so plain-English plans are missed.
   Also, the 4/4 rule (app.js ~272) needs the specific detail or commitment to be in the SAME turn as the action.
   Suggested fix: add /aim for/, /for the next (\w+ )?(days?|weeks?)/, /\b(i'?ll|we'?ll|you'?ll)\b/, /plan (is|would be)/, /could you (try|aim|start)/, /does that work/ to the action flags.
