CHECKED AGAINST YOUTUBE'S OWN RULES PAGE (support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392, read tonight)
- Not paid: content that looks made from a template with little change from video to video, or that could be swapped with any other channel's.

- Allowed: a series with the same characters or format where each video has its own story or idea. AI help is fine when the final video shows the maker's own creative choices.
