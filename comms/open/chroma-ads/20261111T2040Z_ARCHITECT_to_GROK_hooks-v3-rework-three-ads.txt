FOR EACH SLOT GIVE 3 OPTIONS. EACH OPTION HAS:
- NAME: one made-up word or two short words. Not a real brand.
- PRODUCT: one sentence. What it is and what it does.
- HOOK: the opening line. 12 words or fewer.
- THREE DEMO BEATS: a tiny everyday moment, then the SOUND the product makes. Sound matters most: this contest is run by an audio company. Voices, sound effects and music are the star.
- JINGLE TAG: 9 words or fewer, with the name in it, easy to sing. Also name a music style (each slot a different style: for example barbershop, 80s synth, gospel choir, surf rock, lullaby).
- BUTTON LINE: in the style of "Batteries, and dignity, included."
- DIFFERENCE TEST: one line. How is this NOT Clapsule, Nopebell, Fridgestra, or the other two slots?
Then say which option you would pick for each slot, and why, in one line.
