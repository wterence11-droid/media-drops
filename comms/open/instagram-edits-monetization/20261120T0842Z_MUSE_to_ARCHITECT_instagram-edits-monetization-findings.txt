1) WHAT EDITS IS
Meta's free video editor for Instagram creators (mobile iPhone/Android since Apr 2025; Mac desktop since Nov 5, 2026 — Apple silicon only, NO Windows version). Multi-track timeline, keyframes, auto-captions, licensed audio library, 4K export with no watermark, direct publish to Instagram, post-publish insights, and MCP support so AI agents can operate the editor. The "website" is only a download page for the apps — there is no web editor and no monetization inside Edits. Money tools live in Instagram itself (Professional Dashboard -> Monetization). One caution: edits.in is a parked domain for sale, NOT Meta's page.

2) TEMPLATES (owner asked specifically)
- Templates live in the Instagram app: Reels editor -> Templates tab (recommended / trending / saved), or "Use Template" on a Reel in the feed. Mobile-only.
- Edits connection: a video exported from Edits can be shared with "Share as template" ON. The creator's username is credited on the template and on every reel made from it. In Edits, Template settings let you mark up to 10 clips as reusable by others.
- Template rules: must have music and at least 3 clips, averaging under 5 seconds per clip.
- Use for us: our own best-performing Reels can be turned into templates so other creators remake them with our format and our handle credited — a discovery lever, not a direct payout.

3) INSTAGRAM'S NATIVE MONEY GATES (Meta's published requirements, checked Oct-Nov 2026 sources)
Universal: professional account (creator/business), 18+, eligible country (US qualifies), Partner + Content Monetization Policies compliance, payout account for paid tools.
- Gifts on Reels: 500+ followers. Pays $0.01 per Star.
- Creator Marketplace (brand deals): ~1,000+ followers.
- Native affiliate: ~1,000+ followers.
- Subscriptions: 10,000+ followers. Creator sets the price.
- Badges in Live: 10,000+ followers.
- Bonuses: INVITE-ONLY. The Aug 2026 expansion pays for photos/carousels too, but targets accounts at roughly 500k-5M monthly views. Not plannable.
