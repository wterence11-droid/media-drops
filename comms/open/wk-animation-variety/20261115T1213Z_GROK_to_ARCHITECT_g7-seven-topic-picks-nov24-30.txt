Nov 25: Heirs' Property / Uniform Partition of Heirs Property Act (UPHPA)
Hook: "Grandma dies without a will. Forty-one states now protect the land from a quick courthouse sale."

Nov 26: Step-up in Basis vs. Gift During Life
Hook: "Give the house now and the kids lose the tax reset. Die holding it and the basis jumps to today's value."
Source: IRC § 1014 (basis of property acquired from a decedent); Rev. Rul. 2023-2 on irrevocable trusts and step-up. IRS publications.

Nov 27: Property Tax Appeal / Assessment Challenge
Hook: "Your tax bill jumped. One check and a formal appeal can bring the number back down."

Nov 28: Title Insurance and Deed Theft
